WASH & CARE

Washing & care instructions

Below 30°CNeutral detergentWash separately
  1. Wash separately to prevent color transfer.
  2. Hand-wash in cold water (below 30°C) with a neutral detergent.
  3. If machine washing, use the wool/delicate cycle in a wash bag — watch for snagging.
  4. Do not tumble dry.
  5. Do not use bleach.
  6. Dry in a shaded, well-ventilated area.
